A Good Coffee Break

A coffee break can be a small but important part of the day. Some people stop by Tokyo Coffee before university or work, while others come to meet friends, have lunch or simply take a break from a busy day.

Coffee is also a simple way to discover new tastes. You can start with a classic espresso or Americano and later try drinks such as cappuccino, latte, flat white, raf or espresso-tonic.

There are also choices for people who do not drink coffee, including matcha, author tea, lemonades, smoothies and milkshakes.

A simple approach to exploring the menu is: try something familiar, then try something new.

Espresso, Americano and Milk Drinks

Espresso is one of the basic coffee drinks and is also used as the foundation for many other drinks. At Tokyo Coffee, guests can choose classic options such as espresso, Americano, cappuccino and latte.

A flat white is another option for people who prefer a stronger coffee taste with milk. For something different, there are drinks such as raf and espresso-tonic.

When choosing between drinks, think about how strong, milky, sweet or refreshing you want your coffee to be. If you are not sure, asking the barista is a good place to start.

Many espresso drinks can be described simply as espresso + water or espresso + milk.

More Than Coffee

Tokyo Coffee also has a selection of drinks without coffee. Green matcha is available hot or iced, while iced mango-matcha offers a fruitier option.

The author tea menu includes Berry, Pineapple-Passion Fruit, Moroccan, Tashkent and Sea Buckthorn-Ginger. Classic black tea and tea with milk are also available.

For something cold and fruity, guests can choose smoothies and lemonades. Milkshakes are available in vanilla, strawberry, banana and chocolate flavours.

Coffee Terms

Here are a few simple terms that can help when exploring coffee and other drinks.

Espresso
A concentrated coffee drink that is used on its own or as the base for many other coffee drinks.
Americano
A coffee drink made by combining espresso with water.
Flat White
A coffee drink made with espresso and steamed milk.
Matcha
Finely ground green tea powder used to prepare a green tea drink.
