A Good Coffee Break

A coffee break can be a simple way to slow down during a busy day. Some guests prefer a familiar espresso or cappuccino, while others enjoy trying something different.

Choosing a new drink can be a small opportunity to explore another flavour, temperature or preparation style. A useful approach is to try something familiar, then try something new.

Tokyo Coffee offers coffee, tea, matcha and other drinks, so guests can choose something that fits their preferences and mood.

Espresso, Americano and Milk Drinks

Espresso is the base for many coffee drinks. It is a concentrated coffee prepared by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee under pressure.

An Americano is commonly prepared using espresso + water. Adding milk creates a different style of drink, such as a latte or cappuccino. These drinks use espresso + milk but differ in milk texture and proportions.

Guests can choose between different coffee drinks depending on how strong, creamy or mild they want their drink to be.

More Than Coffee

Coffee is not the only type of drink available at Tokyo Coffee. Guests can also explore matcha, different teas, smoothies and lemonades.

Milkshakes and other drinks can also be suitable for guests who prefer something sweet or without coffee.

Exploring different drinks can make each visit different. A guest might choose coffee in the morning and try matcha or another cold drink later.

Coffee Terms

Here are some common terms that can help guests understand different drinks.

Espresso
A concentrated coffee prepared by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee.
Americano
Espresso combined with hot or cold water.
Flat White
An espresso-based drink with textured milk and a relatively thin layer of foam.
Matcha
A powdered green tea traditionally prepared by mixing matcha powder with water or milk.
