How to Make an Enquiry

  1. Choose the type of business enquiry.
  2. Prepare information about your company or organisation.
  3. Describe your expected requirements and approximate volume.
  4. Send the enquiry form and wait for further communication.

Providing clear information helps Tokyo Coffee understand the business request and respond with relevant information.
