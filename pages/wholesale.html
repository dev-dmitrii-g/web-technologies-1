Wholesale & B2B Partnerships

Tokyo Coffee welcomes enquiries from businesses interested in coffee-related cooperation. This page is designed for cafes, restaurants, offices and other organisations that want to discuss a potential business partnership.

A business enquiry can include information about the organisation, expected coffee volume, preferred products and the type of cooperation being considered.

Partnership Areas

Businesses can contact Tokyo Coffee about different types of coffee-related cooperation.

  • Coffee supply enquiries for cafes and restaurants.
  • Coffee supply enquiries for offices and workplaces.
  • Equipment-related enquiries.
  • Barista education and training enquiries.
  • Custom business cooperation proposals.

For a specific request, include useful information in the B2B enquiry form.

Business Enquiries

Information useful for different B2B enquiries

How to Make an Enquiry

  1. Provide your company and contact information.
  2. Choose the type of cooperation you are interested in.
  3. Describe your expected requirements.
  4. Send the enquiry to Tokyo Coffee for review.

Commercial terms, quantities, delivery arrangements and other business conditions should be discussed directly with Tokyo Coffee after the enquiry is received.
