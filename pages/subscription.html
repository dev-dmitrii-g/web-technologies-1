Coffee Subscriptions

Enjoy freshly roasted Tokyo Coffee Co. beans at home with a flexible coffee subscription. Our subscription program is designed for people who want consistently fresh coffee without having to remember to buy another bag every few weeks.

Subscribers can choose between several plans, select whole beans or their preferred grind size, and decide how frequently each delivery should arrive. Coffee selections rotate according to harvest season and availability, giving subscribers an opportunity to discover different origins, producers, processing methods, and flavor profiles.

Before selecting your plan, you can visit our coffee menu and roast catalog to learn more about the tasting profiles of our current coffees. New subscribers can also explore our home brewing guides for recommended ratios, grind settings, and preparation methods.

Subscription Benefits

Freshly Roasted Coffee

Subscription orders are prepared using recently roasted beans so you receive coffee during a suitable freshness window. Each bag includes information about origin, variety, processing method, tasting notes, and recommended brewing style.

Subscriber Savings

Subscribers receive preferred pricing compared with purchasing equivalent bags individually. Selected plans may also include subscriber-only coffees, early access to seasonal releases, or invitations to selected coffee events.

Flexible Delivery

Choose a delivery schedule that matches your coffee consumption. You can select deliveries every two weeks or every four weeks. Grind preferences can also be selected according to your main brewing method.

Save With Regular Delivery

Purchasing coffee through a subscription can reduce the average cost per bag compared with individual purchases. The exact savings depend on your selected plan, delivery frequency, and coffee type. Subscribers also avoid the inconvenience of running out of fresh beans between café visits.

For the best results from each delivery, store coffee in a sealed container away from direct sunlight, moisture, and heat. Grind only the amount required immediately before brewing whenever possible. Our brewing guides contain detailed instructions for V60, French Press, and AeroPress preparation.
