Public Cupping

Coffee cupping is a simple way to compare different coffees by smelling and tasting them under similar conditions.

Participants can notice differences in aroma, acidity, body, sweetness and aftertaste.

Basic session

  1. Smell the dry coffee grounds.
  2. Observe the aroma after adding hot water.
  3. Taste the brewed coffee.
  4. Compare the characteristics of different coffees.
