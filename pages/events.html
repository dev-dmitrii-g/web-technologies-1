Public Cupping

What Is Coffee Cupping?

Coffee cupping is a simple way to compare different coffees by smelling and tasting them under similar conditions.

Participants can notice differences in aroma, acidity, body, sweetness and aftertaste.

A basic cupping session can include the following steps:

  1. Smell the dry coffee grounds.
  2. Observe the aroma after adding hot water.
  3. Taste the brewed coffee.
  4. Compare the characteristics of different coffees.

Latte Art

Milk and Pouring Practice

Latte art practice focuses on preparing espresso-based drinks, creating smooth milk texture and controlling the pouring motion.

Participants can practise the following skills:

  • Prepare an espresso-based drink.
  • Create smooth and consistent milk texture.
  • Control pitcher position.
  • Adjust pouring speed and height.
  • Practise a simple latte art pattern.

Home brewers can also review our Home Brewing Guides for additional coffee preparation information.
