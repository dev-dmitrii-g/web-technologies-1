Public Cupping

What Is Coffee Cupping?

Coffee cupping is a structured method of tasting coffee. It allows people to compare different coffees by looking at their aroma, acidity, sweetness, body and aftertaste. Cupping can help guests notice how different coffees can have different flavours and aromas.

A simple tasting process can include these steps:

  1. Smell the dry coffee grounds.
  2. Observe the aroma after adding hot water.
  3. Taste the brewed coffee.
  4. Compare the characteristics of different coffees.

Cupping is useful for both people who are new to specialty coffee and people who already enjoy exploring different beans.

Latte Art

Milk and Pouring Practice

Latte art combines espresso, textured milk and controlled pouring. The quality of the milk texture is important because it affects how easily a pattern can be created on the surface of the drink.

Beginners can start with basic pouring exercises before attempting more detailed patterns.

  • Prepare an espresso-based drink.
  • Learn how to create smooth milk texture.
  • Control the position of the milk pitcher.
  • Practice the speed and height of the pour.
  • Try a simple pattern.
